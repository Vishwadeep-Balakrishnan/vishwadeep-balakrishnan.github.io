About

Questions worth
spending a life on.

I'm interested in the questions that sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, capital allocation, and the long-term trajectory of civilisation. Not in the abstract — in the concrete and the operational. Who funds what? Who builds what? What survives contact with reality, and what doesn't?

Research & Philanthropy

I conduct independent AI research, focused on the structural questions that are hardest to work on inside a company with a product roadmap: alignment, oversight mechanisms, and the governance of increasingly capable systems.

I also run a philanthropy organisation grounded in hits-based giving — the idea that philanthropic impact, like venture returns, is deeply skewed. Most interventions do little. A small number matter enormously. The goal is to find and fund those.

Writing & Investing

I write about global macro and technology investing on Substack. My focus is on structural themes — how interest rate regimes shape valuations, how dollar cycles affect emerging market technology, and where durable asymmetries live.
